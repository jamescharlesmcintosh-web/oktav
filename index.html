UNIT 01

VECTOR-9

QUADRATURE OSCILLATOR · 2024

A quadrature oscillator built around a single analog core that refuses to be a digital imitation of one. Four phases leave the front panel at once — 0°, 90°, 180°, 270° — and they stay locked to each other under load, under temperature, and under the kind of patching that would make a lesser core give up.

RANGE
0.001 Hz — 22 kHz
CORE
Discrete analog, no DSP
OUTPUTS
4 × quadrature, DC coupled
THD
< 0.02% @ 1 kHz
DRIFT
±3 cents over 0–45 °C
WIDTH
16 HP

Most oscillators give you a waveform. This one gives you a relationship — four points on a circle, moving together. Patch two of them into a ring modulator and the circle turns into a surface. Patch all four into a vector mixer and the surface turns into a body you can steer with one hand.

UNIT 02

KERNEL

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The rack is not a collection of modules. It is one argument, made in voltage, and the patch cable is the sentence structure.

  1. I

    VOLTAGE IS MEANING

    Every jack states its own units on the panel. Nothing is hidden behind a menu, a firmware mode, or a colour you have to memorise. If it carries current, it carries a name.

  2. II

    PATCHING IS AUTHORSHIP

    The instrument does not have presets. It has a history of cables. When you unplug, the state is gone — which is the point. You keep the diagram, not the file.

  3. III

    RETURN IS PART OF THE SOUND

    What a signal does on its way back to zero is as composed as what it does on the way out. We tune the discharge, not just the attack.
